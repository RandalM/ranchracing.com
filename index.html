// Services

What we build

From a single sub-harness to a full chassis loom and the electronics that plug into it.

01

Custom wiring harnesses

Engine, chassis, and dash harnesses built to your car and your layout: engine swaps, EFI conversions, and full race looms, labeled and documented.

02

Custom PCB & electronics design

Purpose-built control boards, sensor interfaces, and microcontroller firmware for the jobs off-the-shelf parts don't cover.

03

Repair & diagnostics

Intermittent faults, melted connectors, mystery drains, and previous-owner wiring. We trace it, fix it properly, and tell you what we found.

04

Low-cost electronics for classic & race cars

Affordable gauges, controllers, and add-on modules designed for vintage and track cars. Modern function, sensible price.
